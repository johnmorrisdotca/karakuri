# Stretch Grabber

A stretchy arm that grows out of a base. Take hold of its tip with a finger or the mouse and lead it round the pegs and walls to the star,
without touching anything red. Written here from the rules alone.

## Rules

- The arm is the path its **tip** has taken from the base. The tip is a disc: it cannot enter a peg or a wall, so it slides along them and the arm bends round.
- The arm can only **stretch** so far (the meter in the bar says how much is left). Bring the tip back along the arm and it draws back in, giving the length back.
- **Win:** the tip touches the star.
- **Loss:** the tip, or any part of the arm, touches a **red hazard** (a spiked red blob or a laser beam). It is at once: there is no second try inside a level.
- Nothing runs on a clock. The arm moves only when the tip is led, so the same path of the finger or mouse always gives the same arm, however slowly it is drawn.
- Let go and the arm stays where it is; take hold of the tip again to go on.

## Levels

Five levels. Each was laid out by hand and the **shortest route** for the tip to the star was found by a search over the free space; the arm is given that
length plus a little slack, which gets less at each level, so the later levels have to be led along nearly the shortest way. The route is recorded as the level's solution, and the
tests play it on the rules. A way to lose each level is recorded too.

| Level | What is in the way |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | A wall with a gap at one end, and a laser to stay away from. |
| 2 | A wall, two pegs to wind round, and two red blobs. |
| 3 | Three laser beams to weave through, and a short wall. |
| 4 | A tall wall to go up round, a peg, a blob and two lasers. |
| 5 | A zigzag of four walls with three laser stubs, and hardly any arm to spare. |

## What is tested

- The tip slides along a wall and a peg and does not enter them, and cannot leave the play area.
- The arm stretches no further than its length, and draws in when the tip comes back along it.
- Touching a blob or a beam loses at once, with the tip or with the side of the arm; touching the star wins.
- The recorded route wins each level, the recorded loss loses, and the same path always gives the same arm.
